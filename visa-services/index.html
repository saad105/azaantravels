Common Documents You May Need

The exact checklist depends on the destination and visa category. Commonly requested documents may include:

  • Valid passport
  • Recent photographs
  • Completed application
  • Travel itinerary
  • Accommodation details
  • Financial documents
  • Sponsor or invitation details
  • Employment or study documents
Important: This is a general checklist, not a guarantee of requirements. Always confirm the latest applicable documents before applying.
