Our Story

Helping People Travel With Greater Confidence

Azaan International Tours & Travels was built around a simple belief: travel planning should feel clear, reliable and personal. From our Nizamabad office, we assist customers with domestic and international journeys, visa-related guidance, Hajj and Umrah arrangements and selected documentation services.

Every customer has different travel dates, budgets and requirements. That is why we focus on listening first, explaining the available options and helping each traveller make an informed decision.

Meet Our Owner

Shaik Majid

OwnerTravel Professional8+ Years of Experience

Shaik Majid is the owner of Azaan International Tours & Travels and brings more than 8 years of experience in the travel and related services industry.

His experience helps customers receive practical guidance for tour planning, air travel, visa-related requirements and pilgrimage arrangements. He believes that trust is earned through honest communication, careful attention and dependable service.

Under his leadership, Azaan Travels continues to support individuals, families and groups from Nizamabad and nearby areas with a personal, customer-focused approach.
