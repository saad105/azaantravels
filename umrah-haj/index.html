Before You Travel

Prepare for Your Journey

Requirements depend on the pilgrimage type, official regulations and selected package. Common preparation items may include:

  • Valid passport
  • Recent photographs
  • Required health documents
  • Applicable visa documents
  • Confirmed travel schedule
  • Emergency contact details
  • Prescribed medicines
  • Essential personal items
Important: Health, vaccination, visa and Hajj eligibility rules can change. Always confirm the latest official requirements before travel.
